Incubation protocol

Explore broadly. Graduate deliberately.

IntelliStream is not a fourth runtime layer. It is the side rail that lets early work cross layers before ownership becomes stable.

01 / EXPLOREResearch question

Validate the problem, collaborators, and cross-layer opportunity.

02 / STABILIZEPublic abstraction

Name the boundary, maintainers, interfaces, and evaluation contract.

03 / VERIFYReproducible path

Document setup, tests, licensing, releases, and sustainable ownership.

04 / GRADUATEDurable home

Move to RIDE Lab, DataSys, or vLLM-HUST according to technical scope.

Shared flagship product

SAGE connects the ecosystem.

Streaming-Augmented Generative Execution applies streaming-computing principles to LLM inference and agent execution. RIDE Lab stewards the core; DataSys and vLLM-HUST retain their own data-system and inference boundaries.

01 / VISIONStream-first execution

Make dataflow, state, backpressure, and continuous execution explicit.

02 / STEWARDRIDE Lab

Maintains the SAGE core repositories and agent-facing product surfaces.

03 / DATADataSys

Owns framework-neutral stream, graph, vector, index, and evolving-data systems.

04 / INFERvLLM-HUST

Owns model execution, scheduling, compilation, kernels, and hardware backends.
